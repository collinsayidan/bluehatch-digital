# BlueHatch Digital — Launch Checklist

## 1. Brand & business
- [ ] Confirm BlueHatch Digital name with Companies House / UK IPO trademark searches and competing market use.
- [ ] Confirm and purchase a suitable domain.
- [ ] Decide on sole trader / company structure, accounting and VAT position (including cross-border digital sales).
- [ ] Update any business identity details and contact disclosures required by law.

## 2. Products & Payhip
- [ ] Finish and quality-check each digital product actually intended for sale.
- [ ] Confirm rights to commercial usage of Canva templates, assets, fonts and third-party material.
- [ ] Check AI content for accuracy, originality and misleading claims.
- [ ] Set explicit product licence terms and accurate file-format descriptions.
- [ ] Upload each final downloadable product to Payhip and configure payment/delivery.
- [ ] Copy each public Payhip checkout URL into `public/assets/js/config.js`.
- [ ] Complete a test purchase and verify payment, confirmation email and digital delivery.
- [ ] Verify GBP pricing, refunds and applicable taxes at checkout.

## 3. Public identity & legal
- [ ] Set the actual business support email in `public/assets/js/config.js`.
- [ ] Configure and test Payhip/Etsy/social links if relevant.
- [ ] Finalise privacy policy for actual analytics, hosts, email tools, and processors.
- [ ] Finalise consumer terms and cancellation/refund process for digital downloads.
- [ ] Add and verify required trading identity and contact information.
- [ ] Add cookie consent **if** non-essential cookies/tracking tools are introduced.
- [ ] Update copyright year when appropriate.

## 4. Domains, SEO & deployment
- [ ] Choose primary deployment: Vercel **or** Coolify for your main domain.
- [ ] Deploy and test on HTTPS and mobile devices.
- [ ] Add canonical domain + absolute OG image URL before requesting social shares.
- [ ] Create domain-based `sitemap.xml` and add it to `public/robots.txt` when domain is live.
- [ ] Test all links, forms, navigation, checkout and downloads.
- [ ] Set up privacy-respecting analytics if wanted and reflect it in legal disclosures.
- [ ] Connect Google Search Console or equivalent after launch if desired.

## 5. Growth
- [ ] Create Etsy listings for any products suitable for Etsy's policies.
- [ ] Set up a real email list service before advertising a newsletter or waitlist.
- [ ] Prepare product launch graphics and short-form social content.
- [ ] Collect genuine customer feedback after launching; do not fabricate testimonials.
