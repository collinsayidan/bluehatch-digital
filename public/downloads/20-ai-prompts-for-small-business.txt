20 Practical AI Prompts for Small Business Owners
Smart Tools. Better Business.

HOW TO USE
Copy any prompt into your preferred AI assistant. Replace details inside
[square brackets] with information about your business. Check all output
before using it, particularly facts, pricing and customer-facing claims.

MARKETING & CONTENT

01. CUSTOMER PROFILE
"Help me create a simple customer profile for my [type of business]
serving [area/audience]. Include their main needs, what might stop them
from buying, and what they value most. Ask me questions if needed."

02. CONTENT IDEAS
"Generate 15 social media post ideas for my [business]. Organise them
into educational, behind-the-scenes, trust-building and promotional posts.
Keep them realistic for a solo business owner."

03. INSTAGRAM CAPTION
"Write three friendly Instagram caption options about [topic] for
[audience]. Use my brand tone: [three adjectives]. End with a gentle,
clear call to action. Avoid exaggerated promises."

04. CONTENT CALENDAR
"Create a four-week social media calendar for [business] with three
posts per week. Include theme, suggested format, key message and CTA."

05. SHORT VIDEO SCRIPT
"Write a 30-second video script showing how [service/product] solves
[customer problem]. Use a natural, conversational tone and include
simple filming instructions."

06. OFFER DESCRIPTION
"Improve this product or service description: [paste text]. Make it
clearer and more benefit-focused without inventing features or results."

07. EMAIL SUBJECTS
"Write 10 useful, non-clickbait email subject lines for [offer/news].
Audience: [audience]. Tone: [tone]."

08. FAQ IDEAS
"Suggest 12 questions potential customers may ask before buying
[service/product]. Do not invent answers; mark where details are needed."

CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION

09. NEW ENQUIRY RESPONSE
"Draft a warm, professional reply to someone enquiring about [service].
Ask only the essential questions needed before offering a quote."

10. FOLLOW-UP MESSAGE
"Write a polite follow-up message for a customer who enquired about
[service] one week ago. Keep it helpful, short and pressure-free."

11. REVIEW REQUEST
"Draft a short and genuine message asking a satisfied customer to
leave an honest review. Make clear that any feedback is welcome."

12. CUSTOMER ONBOARDING
"Create a customer welcome email for new clients of [business]. Include
what happens next, how to prepare, and how to ask questions."

13. EXPLAIN A DELAY
"Write a transparent, empathetic message explaining a delay in [service]
without making excuses or unrealistic promises. Offer practical next steps."

PLANNING & PRODUCTIVITY

14. WEEKLY PRIORITIES
"Act as my planning assistant. Based on these tasks [list], help me
pick three priorities for this week. Explain your choices and suggest
a realistic schedule."

15. REPEATABLE CHECKLIST
"Turn this recurring process [describe process] into a simple numbered
checklist my business can follow consistently."

16. IDEAL WORKFLOW
"Help me map the steps from customer enquiry to completed service in
my [type of business]. Identify common bottlenecks and simple fixes."

17. SMART GOALS
"Turn this vague business goal [goal] into three specific, measurable
and achievable monthly milestones. Ask me for missing context first."

18. PRODUCT IMPROVEMENT
"Act as a thoughtful customer. Review this offer [description] and
point out anything confusing, missing or difficult to understand."

19. COMPETITOR RESEARCH PLAN
"Make a fair competitor research checklist for my [business]. List
public information to compare, but do not invent competitor facts."

20. LEARN A NEW SKILL
"Teach me the basics of [skill] for a small business owner with no
prior experience. Give me a short learning plan and practical exercises."

REMINDER
AI can draft and brainstorm, but it can also make mistakes. Review and
personalise everything before sharing it with customers. Avoid entering
private customer information or confidential business information into
an AI tool without appropriate safeguards.
